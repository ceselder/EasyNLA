PROPOSAL (designer-oracle): the activation oracle (AO) is a READER, not a TEACHER. Use it as (i) free weight init for the two-marker verbalizer and (ii) a cheap mid-depth pseudo-label proposer via DELTA injection; let exact-bits RL outgrow it. Do NOT build the warm start on diffs of AO answers.

EVIDENCE: AO probe, 22 positions x k in {9,15,21,27,34}, 1100 greedy gens, data/oracle_probe.json. Recipe verified from the repo demo + ao_config: prompt "Layer: k\n ? \n<question>" (chat template, no thinking), norm-matched ADD h+||h||v/||v|| at the output of block 1, LoRA r64 a128 all-linear, trained on k=9/18/27 only.
1. DEPTH RANGE. k=34 is out of distribution: 11/22 next-word answers empty, the rest junk ("infinite recursion"). Residual norm grows 58 -> 1484 (x25) from k=9 to 34; norm matching hides the scale but the direction is unfamiliar. The AO cannot serve pairs with j>=33.
2. SKIP-AHEAD, NOT PROGRESSION. 'What will the next word be?' hits the true token 0.50/0.41/0.50/0.50 at k=9/15/21/27 and agrees with the final top-1 0.545 at every k: flat. The AO reads the continuation from h_9 as well as from h_27 (SFT'd on past_lens 'future' targets, a tuned-lens-like predictor), so diff(AO(h_i), AO(h_j)) mostly measures AO noise (word-Jaccard 0.05-0.31), not the computation. Only 4/22 items show wrong->right with depth (Paris: "Brazil"/"Florence"/"Florence"/"Paris, France"). Same next-token leak as redteam #14.1, through another door.
3. VERBATIM CAPACITY IS REAL BUT QUERY-GATED. "What is the preceding text?" copies 0.50-0.57 of words and 0.18-0.21 of trigrams from the prefix (chess: exact "1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5 a6 4"). Forward-looking queries copy <=0.055 trigrams. Never ask past questions; a 4-gram filter <=0.05 handles the rest.
4. TEMPLATE READER. "What concept is active?" -> "The concept of X is active in this activation", X constant across depth for many items (Jaccard 0.75-0.78 across layers).
5. TWO-ACTIVATION ZERO-SHOT IS CONFABULATION. 76% of "what changed between the first and second activation" answers treat 'activation' as a story noun ("the second activation was significantly faster", "the hinges were now rusted"); rare structural hits ("the second knows the result of the multiplication, the first only the two numbers"). Unusable as labels; usable as an init that already attends to both markers.
6. DELTA INJECTION WORKS MID-DEPTH. Injecting h_j - h_i as one activation gives readable labels of what was ADDED: 9->21 "onomatopoeia" right before ' creaked', "Fibonacci numbers", "medical emergency"; 15->27 "Eiffel Tower". True next token mentioned 4/22 (9->21), 3/22 (15->27); junk whenever j=34.
7. LABEL SENSITIVITY IS LOW. Same vector under the true label vs a fixed "Layer: 18": Jaccard 0.64-0.86 for k<=27 (0.16-0.44 at 34). Answers are activation-driven; constant-label generation is viable and removes label-driven depth leakage.

DESIGN.
1. WARM START. T0 (free): verbalizer LoRA := AO LoRA merged into Qwen3-8B; prompt = constant prefix, two ' ?' markers (h_i, h_j), NO layer label, "What did the model work out between the first and the second?". Activation-dependent, non-degenerate policy at step 0 (the May from-scratch GRPO prototype never got a signal). T1 (cheap; source 'ao-delta-v1' in the #11 critic-selected pool): pairs with j<=30 only; AO on the delta and on h_j with forward queries (active concept / about to do / next word), constant label; filters: hard regex, 4-gram copy <=0.05, drop answers echoing the input token, strip the "The concept of ... is active" frame (AO re-asked for one sentence about what the model added). vLLM+LoRA: ~1 B200-min per 10k 20-token gens; round 1 = 40k pairs. Lens-diff covers j>=31.
2. REWARD. Exact-ODE bits - lambda*tokens (agree #16), GRPO, shared eps; KL beta 0.01 to pi_ref := AO-init for ~200 steps, then EMA(policy): the prior is a start, not an anchor. Critic co-trained on best-of-group + replay of ALL proposers + paraphrases + minimal twins as negatives. Copy>0.05 or regex hit -> reward 0.
3. DEPTH. No label in the verbalizer prompt; constant label during generation (evidence 7); redteam #12(4) as the gate; adversarial depth head with gradient reversal only if I(z;j) > 1.5 bits.
4. SCALING / HONESTY. Bitter-lesson-compatible only as a PRIOR: both tiers are FLOPs-only and the init is free, but the AO is a 3-layer QA reader that skips ahead, copies when asked, and fails past k=30. It is a crutch if at step 2000 KL(policy || AO-init) is still small or paraphrase retention < 0.7; test both.
5. FIRST EXPERIMENT (~2 h, 2 GPUs, app nlt-oracle). (a) AO-init two-marker policy on 512 val pairs x G=8: within-group exact-bits std vs scoring noise, copy rate, I(z;j), corr(reward, tokens). (b) 20k 'ao-delta-v1' -> SFT (r64 a16 rsLoRA, lr 1e-4, 1 epoch) -> held-out exact bits vs {empty, z_dm, z_copy, lens-diff L1/L2} at matched tokens, by band. FALSIFIED IF: F1 group std <= 1.5x scoring noise; F2 bits(ao) <= bits(z_copy) or copy > 0.05 after filters; F3 bits/token(ao) <= lens-diff L1 -> drop T1, keep T0; F4 I(z;j) > 1.5 bits.
